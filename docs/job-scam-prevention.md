# Job scam prevention in Nigeria

This guide lists common scam signals, what to avoid, and where to report suspicious recruitment activity. It is intended for researchers and job seekers who need a neutral checklist.

## Practical scam signals
- Requests for upfront payments, “processing fees,” or bank transfers.
- Emails from free or mismatched domains that do not match the organization’s official website.
- Unverifiable claims about guaranteed hiring, immediate offers, or unusual urgency.
- Recruitment announcements that only appear on social media with no official portal notice.
- Requests for sensitive documents (BVN, bank PINs, or passwords).

## What NOT to trust
- Third-party agents that ask for cash payments to “secure” a job.
- Portals that lack a physical address, official contact details, or verified email domains.
- Offers that bypass standard recruitment processes or interviews.

## Reporting channels
- **Nigeria Police Force (NPF)** — report cybercrime or fraud complaints. https://www.npf.gov.ng/
- **Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC)** — report suspected recruitment fraud. https://www.efcc.gov.ng/
- **Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC)** — report deceptive practices. https://www.fccpc.gov.ng/
