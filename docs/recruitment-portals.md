# Nigerian recruitment portals (official)

This document lists official recruitment portals and outlines a checklist for verifying recruitment links. It is meant for researchers, journalists, and job seekers who want a reliable way to confirm a portal’s authenticity.

## Curated list of official recruitment portals
- **Federal Civil Service Commission (FCSC)** — National public service recruitment and vacancy announcements. https://fedcivilservice.gov.ng/
- **Federal Ministry of Labour and Employment** — Labour policy announcements and public notices. https://labour.gov.ng/
- **Nigeria Police Force (NPF)** — Official recruitment notices and verification channels. https://www.npf.gov.ng/
- **Nigerian Armed Forces** — Defence recruitment notices and official guidance. https://www.defence.gov.ng/
- **National Directorate of Employment (NDE)** — Employment programs and public job initiatives. https://nde.gov.ng/
- **National Population Commission (NPC)** — Public recruitment for census and field operations. https://www.nationalpopulation.gov.ng/

## Verification checklist for recruitment links
- Confirm the domain belongs to the official institution (check the homepage and verified contact details).
- Validate that the announcement is published on the institution’s website, not only on social media.
- Look for a matching press release or official notice about the recruitment window.
- Check for official email domains (no free email services for recruitment contact).
- Avoid requests for payments, “processing fees,” or bank transfers.
- Cross-check with national agencies such as the EFCC or NPF if a listing looks suspicious.
