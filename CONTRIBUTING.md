# Contributing

Thank you for contributing to this research repository. Please keep additions neutral, well-sourced, and aligned with the goals of open labour market research in Nigeria.

## How to contribute
1. Fork the repository and create a feature branch.
2. Add or update content with citations to official sources.
3. Keep language neutral and avoid promotional wording.
4. Open a pull request with a brief summary of changes.

## Content standards
- Use authoritative sources (NBS, ILO, World Bank, and official government portals).
- Avoid publishing personal data or private contact details.
- Do not add marketing content or job advertisements.

## Data submissions
If you add datasets or schemas:
- Include definitions for each field.
- Provide source URLs and collection dates.
- Remove personal identifiers and sensitive fields.

## Code of conduct
By participating, you agree to follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
