# Nigeria Job Market Data & Recruitment Resources

A neutral, open research repository for understanding employment trends, recruitment processes, and labour market data in Nigeria. It curates official sources, documents verification steps for recruitment information, and provides reusable dataset structures for analysis.

## Scope of this repository
- Map the landscape of Nigerian labour market data sources and official recruitment portals.
- Provide practical guidance on verification, documentation, and data hygiene.
- Offer a sample dataset schema that can be used for reproducible research.

## Nigeria labour market overview
Nigeria’s labour market statistics are primarily published by the National Bureau of Statistics (NBS), which releases labour force and employment indicators. The International Labour Organization (ILO) and the World Bank provide comparable datasets and metadata that support cross-country analysis and time-series comparisons for Nigeria. These sources are treated as the baseline references for any analysis in this repository.[1][2][3]

## Official recruitment channels in Nigeria
Official recruitment information is commonly published by government institutions and public agencies on their own websites. Examples include the Federal Civil Service Commission and the Federal Ministry of Labour and Employment. This repository lists verified recruitment portals and a checklist for validating recruitment links in [docs/recruitment-portals.md](docs/recruitment-portals.md).[4][5]

## Recruitment scam prevention checklist
For a practical checklist of red flags, reporting channels, and verification steps, see [docs/job-scam-prevention.md](docs/job-scam-prevention.md). The guidance is based on official advisories and enforcement agencies.[6][7]

## Open datasets overview
The [datasets](datasets/) directory documents a minimal, neutral schema for jobs data and explains the philosophy behind sample data structures. The intent is to encourage transparent data collection, clearly defined fields, and reproducible analysis.

## Methodology & update policy
- Prioritize primary sources (NBS, ILO, World Bank, and official government portals).
- Use secondary sources only for contextual explanation, not for headline statistics.
- Record all source links in a references section for traceability.
- Update entries when official sources change URLs, publishing cycles, or data definitions.

## Limitations & disclaimer
This repository does not provide official statistics or recruitment decisions. It is an open research guide and should not be used as a substitute for official announcements. Source links can change without notice; always verify directly with the originating institution.
