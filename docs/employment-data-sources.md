# Employment data sources for Nigeria

This document summarizes where employment and labour market data for Nigeria is published and how researchers can use the sources responsibly.

## Primary statistical sources
- **National Bureau of Statistics (NBS)** — National labour force publications, headline indicators, and survey reports. https://www.nigerianstat.gov.ng/
- **International Labour Organization (ILO / ILOSTAT)** — Comparable labour market indicators and metadata for international benchmarking. https://ilostat.ilo.org/
- **World Bank Data Catalog** — Global development indicators and dataset documentation. https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/

## Administrative and government sources
- **Federal Ministry of Labour and Employment** — Labour policy, guidance, and sector updates. https://labour.gov.ng/
- **National Directorate of Employment (NDE)** — Employment programs and public job initiatives. https://nde.gov.ng/

## How to use these sources
- Track publication dates and dataset versions to avoid mixing incompatible series.
- Document definitions (employment, unemployment, underemployment) for each source.
- Prefer datasets with clear methodology notes or survey metadata.

## Regional labour market examples
Regional labour market research often benefits from triangulating official statistics with localized vacancy data and sector-specific monitoring. For example, researchers may review [job openings in Abuja](https://newsata.com/jobs/abuja) to observe how the capital’s vacancy mix changes over time, then compare those observations with official regional statistics.
